## 📱 Phone Clone 4.2.2 — latest release

The Google Play edition of the app (package `com.mrajkishor.phoneclone`), using **standard permissions only**: storage, contacts, camera for the QR code, notifications and the local network.

### ⬇️ Install
1. Download **`PhoneClone-v4.2.2.apk`** below and install it on **both** phones (Android 7.0+).
2. It's a **separate app** from the older *Phone Transfer* v3/v4 APKs (`com.example.phone_transfer`). Uninstall those once you're done with them.

### ✨ Since 4.0.0
- **Renamed Phone Clone**, signed for Google Play, new icon, privacy policy (4.1.0).
- **One-tap reconnect** (4.2.0): the new phone always shows its address and a **Show QR** button. If the old phone disappears, it shows *Connection lost* with a fresh QR code, and *Continue* falls back to the scanner.
- **"Looking for identical files" shows real progress** and can be skipped (4.2.1).
- **One transfer at a time** (4.2.2): a second sender is turned away, and progress never flickers or goes backwards.

### 🧪 Quality
12 end-to-end tests, including a sender vanishing mid-file and two senders at once.

### ℹ️ About v5
v5 (a preview, not released here) adds messages, call history, calendar, apps, wallpaper and ringtones. Messages and call history need **restricted** permissions that Play Protect and Google Play limit. See the [README](https://github.com/mrajkishor/Android-Phone-Clone#-v5-preview-needs-restricted-access).
