## 📱 PhoneHop 5.7.0

Move everything from your old Android phone to your new one over Wi-Fi: files, contacts and, new in v5, **messages, call history, calendar, installed apps, wallpaper and ringtones**.

### ⬇️ Install
1. Download **`PhoneHop-v5.7.0.apk`** below and install it on **both** phones (Android 7.0+).
2. ⚠️ Messages and call history need **restricted permissions**, so **Google Play Protect may block the install** when the APK comes from WhatsApp or a browser. Install over USB (`adb install PhoneHop-v5.7.0.apk`), or turn off *Play Protect → Scan apps* while installing and turn it back on afterwards.
3. Prefer standard permissions only? Use [v4.2.2](https://github.com/mrajkishor/Android-Phone-Clone/releases/tag/v4.2.2).

### ✨ New in v5
- **Extras** (each optional, each asks first): messages (SMS & MMS), call history, calendar, installed apps (install with one tap each), wallpaper, ringtones, contact groups. Messages and call history are marked **Restricted**.
- **Files tab** on both phones: every file compared old vs new, grouped by type, folder or status, with thumbnails and app icons, filters and search. Every file has **Open** and **Show in Files app** buttons.
- **Extras tab** with a plain status for each item and **Try again** on both phones.
- Files already on the new phone are skipped, even after a reinstall. Folders Android protects are listed as **Restricted**.
- WhatsApp: its own **Transfer chats** is recommended (no Google Drive needed).

### ✏️ New name
**Phone Clone is now PhoneHop.** It updates in place over Phone Clone 4.1+ and keeps your data and snapshots.

### 🆕 New in 5.6.0
- **Transfer snapshots:** every transfer is kept as a read-only snapshot of its **Progress, Files and Extras** tabs, in *Internal storage › Documents › PhoneHop › Snapshots*. It stays after the old phone is gone, even if the app is uninstalled. Open it from the home screen.
- **Export** a snapshot with the share button (Drive, WhatsApp, email…), and **Import** one from the home screen.
- **Show in Files app** now opens the file's own folder on Moto, Pixel and Samsung phones.

### 🧪 Quality
16 automated tests, including skip-then-retry for extras and a new phone restarted between transfers. Not yet tested end to end on real phones.
