## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```
Phone Clone moves everything from your old Android phone to your new one, directly over Wi-Fi. There's no cloud, no account and no cable.

Helping you move to a new phone is all this app does. To copy every file into the same folder it had on your old phone, it needs "All files access" on both phones.

WHAT IT TRANSFERS
• Photos and videos, with their original dates and albums
• Music, voice recordings and other audio
• Documents: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, e-books
• Archives, APK files and every other file on your phone's storage
• Contacts, including their photos (contacts already on the new phone are skipped)

The app finds everything automatically and groups it by type, so you just switch on what you want.

FAST
• Several files move at once, tuned automatically to your Wi-Fi
• Small files travel in batches, so thousands of photos fly through
• Identical files are sent once and copied on the new phone
• Tip: use your new phone's hotspot for the best speed

EXACT AND SAFE
• Every file is checked with a SHA-256 checksum before it's kept
• Files keep their folders and original dates
• No duplicates: files already on the new phone are skipped
• Your old phone is never changed. The app only reads from it

NEVER STARTS OVER
• Pause and resume from the exact byte where you stopped
• If Wi-Fi drops, it waits and continues automatically
• Keeps running in the background, with progress in the notification
• Files that fail are retried automatically; any that still fail are listed with their location, so you can copy them by hand

PRIVATE
Data goes only from your old phone to your new phone over your own network. No servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

HOW TO USE
1. Install Phone Clone on both phones.
2. Connect both to the same Wi-Fi, or join the new phone's hotspot from the old one.
3. New phone: tap "This is the NEW phone".
4. Old phone: tap "This is the OLD phone" and scan the QR code.
5. Choose what to send and tap Start.

WHAT CAN'T BE TRANSFERRED
Android doesn't let regular apps copy installed apps and their data, SMS, call history, chat histories inside other apps (use their own backup), or system settings.
```
